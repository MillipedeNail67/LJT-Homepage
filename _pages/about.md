I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/HKUST-NLP) led by [Professor Junxian He](https://www.cs.ust.hk/~jhe/) at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://www.ust.hk/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My current research interests include:
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
